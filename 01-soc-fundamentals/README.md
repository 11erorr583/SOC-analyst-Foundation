# Task 01: SOC Fundamentals

**Date:** 12 November 2026  
**Focus:** Cybersecurity introduction and SOC Analyst responsibilities  
**Status:** Completed

## Objectives

- Explain the CIA triad.
- Differentiate threat, vulnerability, and risk.
- Describe the purpose of a Security Operations Center (SOC).
- Compare Level 1, Level 2, and Level 3 analyst responsibilities.
- Distinguish Red Team and Blue Team activities.

## Topics Covered

### CIA Triad
The three foundational security objectives studied were confidentiality, integrity, and availability.

### Threat, Vulnerability, and Risk
The report introduces the distinction between a potential source of harm (threat), a weakness that could be exploited (vulnerability), and the potential for loss when a threat exploits a vulnerability (risk).

### Security Operations Center
The task covers the SOC as a defensive function responsible for monitoring, detecting, analyzing, and responding to cybersecurity threats. It also discusses continuous monitoring and the people, processes, and technology involved.

### SOC Analyst Tiers
The report compares the responsibilities of L1 triage analysts, L2 incident responders, and L3 threat hunters or senior analysts.

### Red Team vs Blue Team
The task compares offensive security testing with defensive security operations, including their mandates and commonly used tools and techniques.
